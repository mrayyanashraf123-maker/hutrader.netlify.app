On the bench

What board-level repair means here

Most shops swap the part. We trace the rail, find what failed, and replace the actual cause.

Rail-by-rail diagnosis

Current measurement on every power rail, thermal camera sweep to catch the short, oscilloscope confirmation before and after repair.

Chip-level rework

BGA reballing of CPU, GPU, PCH and PMIC with infrared and hot-air rework, plus stencil work for individual pads.

BIOS & firmware

SPI programmer for dead boards, BIOS transfer across a board swap, password removal and clean OEM image flashing.

Verified before return

Every board runs a 30-minute burn-in under load with the temperature logged — not just a power-on check.
